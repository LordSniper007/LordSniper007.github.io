04 / FAQ

Before you
get started.

Clear answers about compatibility, local data, Lua workflows and support.

What is VoidTools?＋

VoidTools is a focused Windows workspace that brings maintenance, diagnostics, Lua tooling and everyday technical utilities into one application.

Which versions of Windows are supported?＋

VoidTools provides a 64-bit installer for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

What does the Lua Obfuscator do?＋

Bring in Lua or Luau source, configure compatible transformation stages, review diagnostics and export a protected build from one workflow.

Does VoidTools store my workspace data online?＋

VoidTools has no central database for workspace files, reports or tool inputs. Working data generally stays local unless you choose a feature that contacts an external service.

How do I get help?＋

Contact the team through the official VoidTools Discord. We aim to send an initial response within 24 hours.
